# 🃏 The Neon Fool's Wavelength Picker 🛸

Welcome, seeker of the spectrum! You've stumbled upon the **Neon Color Palette**, a web application so esoteric, it makes a blacklight poster look like a tax return. 

Designed for the inkjet printers of the future (and the blacklight posters of your dreams), this app is your gateway to the vibrant, the fluorescent, and the frankly unnecessary.

## 🌈 The Esoteric Prophecy (Features)

- **Vaporwave Aesthetics**: Experience a dark neon-grid background that scrolls through the digital ether.
- **Matrix Rain of the Cosmos**: Falling emojis of aliens, UFOs, and space icons, because why use numbers when you can use 👽?
- **Inkjet Simulation**: A high-tech "printing" preview that moves back and forth, because we value the journey as much as the destination.
- **The Palette of the Gods**: 15+ curated neon-like colors, each with their own secret HEX and RGB scrolls.
- **Wavelength Slider**: Cross the threshold of light with our interactive spectrum bar.
